About me

Hi! I am Osvaldo, a senior software developer with a deep expertise on biological data modeling, data visualisation and data analysis. I have experience in performing scientific experiments, coding and providing software services. I have created Python packages and R shiny applications that are used worldwide by researchers to better understand their data.
